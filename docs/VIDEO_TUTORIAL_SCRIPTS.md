## Tutorial 1: Getting Started with EduOS

**Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Audience:** New administrators  
**Prerequisites:** None

### Script

**[00:00 - 00:30] Introduction**

"Welcome to EduOS, the comprehensive platform for educational institutions. In this tutorial, we'll walk through the basics of getting started with EduOS. By the end of this video, you'll know how to log in, navigate the dashboard, and understand the key features available to you."

**[00:30 - 01:30] Logging In**

"Let's start by logging into the platform. Navigate to your institution's EduOS URL. This will be either your subdomain, like springfield.eduos.com, or your custom domain if you've set one up.

Enter your email address and password. If this is your first time logging in, you'll be prompted to change your temporary password. Choose a strong password with at least 8 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.

Next, you'll be asked to set up multi-factor authentication, or MFA. This adds an extra layer of security to your account. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, like Google Authenticator or Authy, and enter the 6-digit code to verify."

**[01:30 - 03:00] Dashboard Overview**

"Now you're logged in! Let's explore the dashboard. At the top, you'll see quick stats showing your active students, today's attendance rate, and pending payments.

On the left sidebar, you have the main navigation menu. Here you can access:
- Students: Manage student records
- Attendance: Track and report attendance
- Payments: Handle financial transactions
- Users: Manage staff and permissions
- Hierarchy: Configure your organizational structure
- Schemas: Create dynamic forms
- Settings: Configure your tenant

The center panel shows recent activity, giving you a quick overview of what's happening in your institution. And on the right, you'll see important alerts and notifications."
